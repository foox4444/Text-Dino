This Adventurer Dino. It is going to get more updates, but right now, this is it. I recreated the Chrome Dino game. 
I added birds and cacti as obstacles and made them come at you randomly. The birds have a flapping animation.
The background has some clouds that randomly spawn and a track that you run on.
I made a pause menu open when you press P, and you can use the up and down arrow keys to choose between resume and restart; when you decide to pick one, you press Enter. You can also restart usign R.
The dino can jump and duck. It has running animations for both.
It has a score system and a high score system. Your high score saves on your computer.
My assets come from https://github.com/ImKennyYip/chrome-dinosaur-python.
